THE GAUNTLET

Fourteen days. Six written matches. Miss one and you're eliminated.

Week 1 // The Undercard

Warm up. It doesn't last.

Day 1-2 · Match 1Heat: ■□□□□

THE OPENER

First bell. No warm-up. No mercy.

Reveal the stipulation

Write a 300-word scene that opens with the line: "The lights went out and the whole crowd went silent." Any genre. Post it to the group chat before the second night ends.

Late entries are counted out.

Day 3-5 · Match 2Heat: ■■□□□

THE SCAVENGER RUMBLE

Five fragments. Hidden across campus. Everyone's racing.

Reveal the stipulation

A school-wide scavenger hunt: follow our clues to find five hidden story fragments (library, courtyard, notice boards and other open areas). Piece them together and write the 200-word story they reveal. First clean submission takes the round.

Daytime only. Approved areas only. Walk, don't run.

Day 6-7 · Match 3Heat: ■■■□□

TAG TEAM CHAOS

Your partner is masked. You tag in when the clock says so.

Reveal the stipulation

You're paired with a random, anonymous member. Together you write one 300-word story, but you swap every 5 minutes. No planning. No discussing. Pick up the story where they left it and make it better.

Partners are revealed after the final bell. Maybe.

Week 2 // The Main Card

Now it counts.

Day 8-10 · Match 4Heat: ■■■■□

THE STEEL CAGE

No way in until you solve it. No way out until you submit.

Reveal the stipulation

An escape-room style challenge: crack a chain of ciphers and riddles to unlock your story prompt. Then write a 500-800 word short story built around what you unlocked. Submit by the end of Day 10.

Teams are allowed. Trust is optional.

Day 11-13 · Match 5Heat: ■■■■□

THE PROMO BATTLE

Sixty seconds. One mic. The crowd decides.

Reveal the stipulation

Live, in character: draw a random object from the bag and cut a 60-second story-promo on the spot, hyping your hero or your villain. The pack votes with the loudest crowd reaction. Then read two members' stories and give structured feedback: what worked, what didn't, one suggestion.

Words only. Hype yourself, never put anyone down.

Day 14 · Main EventHeat: ■■■■■

THE TITLE SHOT

Spotlights on. Entrance music. One voice in the ring.

Reveal the stipulation

The final read-aloud: lights down, one spotlight, the whole pack in the room. Make your entrance, step up, and read your best piece out loud. That's your audition. The crowd picks the Champion of the Night.

Come early. Come ready. Masks welcome.

Survive all 6 → You're in the pack. Miss one → Eliminated. No exceptions.

Every match is written. Words only, nobody gets hurt. All activities happen in approved school spaces, with the pack and proper supervision.
